## What the ordered structures actually establish

Both original and lower preserving candidates have the same ordered twelve-atom
endpoints and continuous lift. Qx stays negative while the z structural proxy
reverses. T escape changes the endpoint distortion instead of reaching the
opposite polar variant. Equal phase labels or endpoint energies alone therefore
do not identify an atom-resolved path.

The original preserving center already has individual Hf-relative oxygen y
displacements of about +/-0.225 and +/-0.322 A despite an almost zero mean.
At all nine samples, both preserving chains keep every oxygen inside its
registered Hf layer band; the minimum fractional-z margin is 0.145502.
This also holds for the piecewise-linear fractional segments, not an unknown
smooth path. The lower-minus-original center difference includes staggered
oxygen z displacements around +/-0.200 A and Hf x displacements around
+/-0.068 A after common translation removal. The lower center is a mixed
rearrangement, not the onset of all lateral motion or a newly assigned phonon.

## Supported increment and rival explanation

Within one partial clamp, reconnecting a mixed rearrangement gives lower
preserving candidates at all three existing strains and changes the sampled
0-to-1% penalty from 28.596 to 8.533 meV/HfO2. Matched local controls show
that considerable accommodation remains at fixed cells. Crossing strain
and the retained four-vector yields a -15.973 meV/HfO2 finite interaction;
at +1% the q0 local candidate lies 0.699 above the lower connected sampled peak.
Together these link retained patterns, transverse accommodation, the open
row and candidate mechanical response under a common energy reference.

The serious rival interpretation remains that this is a better-relaxed
member of a known path class. The comparison does not establish a new path
topology, universal mechanical selection rule, predictive reduction or
optimizer advance. The small switching/escape peak gaps do not resolve
global barrier orderings. That limits the JCTC novelty case even after
the mechanism is made readable; adding figures cannot remove the limit.
