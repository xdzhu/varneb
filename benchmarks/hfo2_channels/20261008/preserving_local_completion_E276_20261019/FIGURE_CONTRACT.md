# Matched local accommodation at zero and +1% strain

Purpose: determine how the retained-pattern preserving accommodation
changes with imposed strain, distinguishing atomic release from the
additional joint atomic/open-cell response. Do not presuppose that
the new +1% A/J controls succeed or equal a whole-path barrier change.

Archetype: quantitative2x2grid. Existing Python/matplotlib workflow only.

- (a) F−A and additional A−J local energy lowerings, separately for
  zero-minus, zero-plus, +1%-minus and +1%-plus. No sign averaging.
- (b) Atomic displacement norms F→A/F→J in the original-T metric,
  not per-atom RMS, mass-weighted modes or energy contributions.
- (c) Signed transverse structural-proxy changes F→A/F→J. This
  follows the mixed accommodation, not a named eigenmode or Berry P.
- (d) Open-y response relative to F. A fixes the complete F cell;
  J may involve additional atoms as well as the three open cell DOFs.

Only fully native-replayed, released-gradient-qualified A/J candidates
enter this complete comparison. Missing outcomes refuse the figure,
not zero-filled bars. The old +1% budget failure and the explicitly
revised0.5→1.0A numerical neighborhood remain disclosed. Same four
observable definitions do not mean equal native amplitudes across strain.

183x160mm, editable PDF/SVG and300dpi PNG, normal13pt(a)--(d), top/right
spines/ticks, aligned panels and labels, no subpanel titles, readable
fonts and framed semi-transparent white legends outside the data.
No fit, smoothing, statistical replicates or inferred energy error bars.
CSV rows and native archives identify every plotted point. No pixels
are generated or selectively edited; all visuals derive from data.

Review risks: a numerical radius is not a changed Hamiltonian; a gradient
candidate is not a certified minimum; A−J is not pure elastic energy;
the new local contrast is not subtracted from a NEB sampled peak.
The whole-path context is retained separately, not added to this panel
grid as a putative exact local-to-global energy decomposition.
