# Final cross-control figure contract

Conclusion to test, not assume: the strain response of the local preserving
candidate depends on its retained shuffle state. Four measured local
candidate energies distinguish a strain effect from changing that state.

Quantitative 2x2 grid, existing Python/matplotlib workflow:
(a) all four energies relative to the same-strain initial well;
(b) q1-minus-q0 energy difference at each strain, whose change is I;
(c) mean O-minus-Hf transverse displacement;
(d) permitted open-y stretch in the common original-T frame.
All panels use the same strain x coordinates and aligned subplot boxes.
Lines between two measured conditions are visual guides, not fitted laws.
Two diagonal candidates are reused; two crosses are new, never replicas.

Use the actual completed analysis JSON and CSV only. Missing candidates
block a complete-comparison figure; no interpolated energies or error bars.
No energy is assigned to mode amplitudes, no electronic polarization inferred.

User style overrides defaults: normal-weight (a) labels, larger text,
top/right spines and inward ticks, no subplot titles, framed translucent
white legend outside the data panels, aligned x/y labels. Export PDF/SVG
with editable text plus PNG preview; source JSON/CSV and code accompany it.
