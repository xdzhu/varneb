# Bounded final experiment

Hypothesis: the local energy response to imposed strain depends on the
retained internal shuffle state. Two existing diagonal candidates and two
new crossed candidates separate the factors. This is a two-by-two local
candidate comparison, not an optimizer contest or statistical experiment.

Metric: I=[U(1,q1)-U(1,q0)]-[U(0,q1)-U(0,q0)], in meV/HfO2. Report all
four U values, fixed-q strain responses, fixed-strain pattern contrasts,
released gradients and structural observables. No invented error bars.

Budget: two conditions, 40 attempts each, same six ABACUS physical assets,
two 32-CPU allocations, no further expansion. End on both original handles'
terminal outcomes. Then complete manuscript, figure and case integration.

repository's ordinary test/Slurm tools and retain this finite protocol.
