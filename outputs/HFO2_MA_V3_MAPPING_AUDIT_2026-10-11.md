- Our clamped M candidate cannot be assigned to either author's route just
  because its barrier is numerically close. First compare proper cell-frame
  transport, ordered-atom permutation and continuous periodic lift. Our
  registered T-pattern label and a phase-pair name do not establish that match.
- The current M atomic projection and strain trace remain descriptive geometry
  in the project's declared reference. They are not a new identification of
  the author's mixed shuffle, a mode-energy partition, or proof that an omitted
  direction is physically immobile. Strong T/Cmma controls remain necessary.

  M candidate; do not silently add a second M chain, change G3 selection, read
  the +0.5% holdout or redefine the finite 13-chain budget. Our conclusions
  concern examined candidates, not exhaustive global escape.

  defaults. Ordinary VARNEB remains 0.10 in its declared metric, with the
  original100Ry/full10auDZP contract. Different ordinary criteria do not by

  pristine partial-clamp network. The fixed PBE ensemble and its applicability
  remain explicit; no new functional or defect calculation is requested.
