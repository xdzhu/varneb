## Consequences for our analysis, not corrections of their data

The schematics are not an ordered production-cell mapping, numerical
eigenvector set or mass-normalization certificate. Our reference gauges,
integer lifts and all four Cmma registrations remain unchanged. Their
dispersion plots do not certify the unmeasured movable complement of our
partially clamped bottleneck.

A displayed ordinate, a curve's maximum relative to its own starting state,
and a stationary barrier are different quantities. Different starting
ordinates cannot be compared as though every curve shares a zero initial
energy. We do not digitize these curves, infer a numerical barrier or
assert an error in the authors' barrier interpretation. The distinct
printed unit labels also do not establish the normalization needed to
import polynomial coefficients into our coordinate chart.

Restricted relaxation/profile lowering remains prior work. Our proposed
increment still requires same-boundary switching/escape competition and a
prospectively frozen improvement over the strongest valid controls, beyond
actual numerical resolution. This reading neither supplies that result nor
selects G3 modes, adds a Hessian or changes B0-B5/holdout rules.
