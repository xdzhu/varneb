## Our inference and finite next action

Neither phase identity, large mode weight nor observation of energetic softening
establishes a new predictive theory. Keep the scientific test on the same
initial well and matched boundary: can switching be facilitated without
facilitating the easiest examined escape, and can the response be predicted
beyond strong controls? The reference-mapped Cmma, endpoint and interpolation
comparisons remain necessary. All ordinary G1 chains must finish before G2;
no new functional, domain-wall, finite-temperature or phonon-dispersion matrix
is opened by these papers.

The [connected draft](../paper/VARNEB_JCTC/MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md) now separates
dated measurements from these unmeasured claims. The main plan adds the two
neighbours to its claim boundary without revising the registered experiments.
