# HfO2 scientific acceptance: finite analysis and manuscript closure

The author requested a new goal after the final two crossed calculations
completed. Completing that calculation protocol did not establish JCTC
readiness. This goal closes the scientific assessment, including an honest
negative journal recommendation if the demonstrated increment is insufficient.

## Question and locked terminology

How does the atomic accommodation permitted by a partially clamped plane
change the strain response of reversal relative to escape from the polar well?

Preserving/reversing denote the sign of a geometric reference shuffle, not
a published path-family assignment. Qx/Qy/Qz remain geometric projections;
q0/q1 are retained four-observable vectors. The transverse species-mean
displacement is not a phonon eigenvector or electronic polarization.
Original x/z cell rows are prescribed and the original y row can respond.
The ordinary NEB target stays 0.10 eV/A. No DFT setting or completed label changes.

## Three concrete deliverables

1. A primary-source comparison of ordered endpoints, atomic-route features,
   mechanical constraints and claim overlap. State partial correspondences
   explicitly when published atom mappings cannot be matched from the available
   structures. Do not assign a complete literature family by one projection.
2. An atom-resolved main figure using the archived original/lower centers and
   complete bands, linked to the final local crossed states. Show staggered
   oxygen motion and Hf motion, and their connection to the allowed cell
   response and energies. Keep the original quantitative evidence accessible.
3. A revised argument, compiled reading manuscript, concise Chinese guide
   and three-emphasis pre-submission assessment with a definite JCTC
   recommendation and explicit remaining evidence gaps.

The paper retains its four approved Results sections. The old local-release
overview may move to an in-article appendix when the atom-resolved mechanism
figure takes its main-text place. Existing numerical tables and figure data
are retained. No supplementary-information package is introduced.

## Scope and completion rule

Use existing native data and the established Python figure workflow. No new
material, strain grid, mode fit, Hessian or path calculation is needed for
these deliverables. No submission or release is requested. Preserve the
five unrelated user edits and other sessions' untracked work.

Completion means the comparison, figure and readable argument have been
delivered, checks and compilation succeeded, the journal recommendation
has been stated with its reasons, and the work has been pushed. It does
not mean that a journal has accepted the work or that unsupported stronger
claims have become true. If originality remains inadequate, report that
outcome and the specific gap rather than silently starting more calculations.
