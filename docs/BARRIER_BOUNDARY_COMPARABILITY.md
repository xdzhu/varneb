# Barrier comparisons require an explicit endpoint and potential contract

2026-10-12. This clarifies the existing HfO2 protocol, not a new algorithm,
physical hypothesis, material result or additional calculation budget.

For identical endpoint geometries I/F and one potential, a restricted set of
continuous paths is a subset of the unrestricted set. Its ideal minimax
barrier cannot be smaller. This does not order local finite-image NEB answers.
It also does not order barriers from newly relaxed endpoints under different
substrates: their initial-well subtraction changes. Absolute bottleneck energy,
well energy and their difference must be distinguished.

Terminology follows the manuscript: IS/FS are physical endpoint states;
epsilon is the actual prescribed biaxial substrate strain; B-star is an ideal
minimax barrier, not a sampled maximum. The Appendix B coordinates x/y are
dimensionless illustrative variables, not HfO2 modes or the +0.005 holdout.

The existing B0/B1 controls and H1 delta=0 test remain unchanged. Free G1 and
zero-strain clamped G2 are different boundary ensembles. Both G2 strain labels
use the same clamping definition, but not identical endpoint geometries.
Comparisons require their own common-PO initial reference within each condition.
No stationarity, saddle index or sampling error is certified by this note.

## Primary-source recheck and limits

[Behara--Van der Ven's public accepted manuscript](https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.6.054403),
printed pp.2/7/8, is visually rechecked from the pinned 14-page PDF.
Equation1 defines strain components relative to cubic axes, not our prescribed
T-substrate epsilon. TableI and Figure10 distinguish routes with identical
shuffle signs; sign agreement does not establish a unique path. III.D fixes
the entire orthorhombic cell, whereas Figure12 introduces a separate planar
boundary thermodynamic construction with neglected shear. Neither is our
three-open-component homogeneous clamped calculation. The manuscript's
relaxed-path lower-bound statement is not evidence for a monotonic barrier
response when our relaxed endpoints change. The last sentence is our
comparison inference, not a claim attributed to the authors.

The publisher still marks its SI subscription-required; the author entry
has no retrieved attachment. SI remains unreviewed. This recheck closes no
SI gap, imports no author coordinates or numeric barriers, changes no physical
input or source, and admits no extra variant, G3 centre or holdout.
