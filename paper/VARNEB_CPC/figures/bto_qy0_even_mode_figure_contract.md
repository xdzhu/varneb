# BTO restricted-mode-sheet figure contract

- **Core conclusion:** A (Q_y=0) symmetry-restricted, variable-cell BTO (Q_z,Q_x) energy sheet connects the projected T and C endpoint coordinates, while a nine-node even-mode fit predicts two independently computed interior energies within the prespecified 2 meV/BTO gate.
- **Archetype:** Quantitative grid, with the measured-domain contour as the hero panel and prospective prediction residuals as the validation panel.
- **Backend:** The repository's existing Python/matplotlib manuscript-figure workflow, exclusively.
- **Target/export:** CPC two-column width, approximately 183 mm; editable PDF and SVG plus review PNG, with source-data CSV and machine-readable QA. Retain 600-dpi TIFF locally if needed for submission.
- **Panel (a):** (E-E_C) model contour only inside the convex hull of nine audited training nodes and the separately audited T endpoint; show every actual training node, the two genuinely prospective holdouts, C, T, and the projected seven-image VCNEB path at (Q_x\simeq0). The endpoint is not a model-training datum.
- **Panel (b):** Model-minus-observation residuals for the two prospective points, with the predeclared \(\pm2\) meV/BTO gate. Earlier points and the T endpoint may be shown in a quieter style but must be marked retrospective.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** Raw-audited ABACUS energies/forces/stress and frozen-before-DFT model predictions outrank the smooth contour; the contour is a compact visualization of the fitted model, not additional DFT data.
- **Statistics:** This is deterministic electronic-structure sampling, not a statistical replicate study. State training-node count, prospective holdout count, maximum absolute blind error, force/stress gates, and interpolation domain; do not invent error bars.
- **Source data:** Audited nine-node patch, frozen coefficient/prediction JSON, prospective raw-audit gate, archived T/C endpoint static audit, and archived path-mode projection, each hash-bound in the QA record.
- **Image integrity:** Mark measured coordinates over any interpolated shading; do not fill outside the measured convex hull, smooth away failures, or rescale energies per cell without disclosing one-BTO-formula-unit normalization. The path is a projection into these two coordinates; it is not constrained to the two atomic eigenvectors because stable atomic modes and strain relax.
- **Reviewer risk:** The third unstable cubic mode is deliberately fixed at (Q_y=0), so this is *not* the globally minimized two-coordinate PES. Two holdouts do not prove branch stability or global interpolation accuracy; the T endpoint is a separately optimized near-stationary endpoint, not a newly relaxed conditional-grid point. Do not call the BTO T→C monotonic profile an activation barrier.
- **Style:** White background, non-bold 11-pt `(a)` and `(b)`, visible upper/right axis spines, aligned panel x axes, legible ticks/labels, framed semitransparent white legends placed away from curves, no subplot titles.
