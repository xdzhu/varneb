# First converged ordinary T--PO band: figure contract

Core conclusion: ordinary residual convergence and a compact geometric
description of the sampled maximum do not establish that a fixed few-mode
representation spans the entire T--PO path.

Archetype: quantitative grid, one audited chain, six aligned panels. Python
matplotlib is the existing project plotting backend; no R or AI imagery.
This is an evidence figure for the JCTC research draft, not a claim that the
manuscript or a full-variable saddle has been certified.

## Evidence map

- (a) P=0 discrete energy profile in meV per HfO2 formula unit, relative to T.
  Dots are actual completed SCFs; straight connections only guide the eye.
  The maximum is a sampled maximum, not a labelled TS.
- (b) Three geometric rotated-T-pattern amplitudes in Cartesian Angstrom.
  Their construction is unchanged; do not attach unverified irrep labels.
- (c) Diagonal Green strains relative to the original ordered T reference.
  Strain is separate from atomic displacement, not silently rotated away.
- (d) Squared atomic-displacement norm captured by that complete triplet.
  The parent-scaffold reference and unweighted metric differ from panel(e).
- (e) Squared mass-weighted optical displacement fractions relative to T,
  grouped into complete degenerate-frequency subspaces. Show the two groups
  largest at the sampled maximum and their complement. Selection is explicitly
  descriptive, not an independent prediction. The fraction at T is undefined
  because its reference displacement is zero; leave it absent, not zero.
- (f) Atomic and scaled-cell ordinary-NEB maximum-vector residuals for moving
  images. Fixed endpoints have no NEB residual value. The0.10 eV/Angstrom
  threshold is not a TS stationarity condition.

Reviewer risks: confusing mode weights with energy partitions; confusing T
reference frequencies with local path phonons; identifying the highest image
with the optimization bottleneck; treating reference subspace coverage as
proof of conditional stability, uniqueness or global channel optimality.
Do not use fitted extrema, error bars without measurements, or individual
vectors inside a degenerate group. No statistical replicates are implied.
